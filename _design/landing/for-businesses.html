Is the free report actually free?
Yes. No card, no sales call, no signup before you see it. Type your business name, we pull your data, you see the report on one page. Save it, share it, come back later. Static snapshot — paid plans refresh weekly.
Where do you get the patient data?
Public Google and Yelp reviews. We don't see anyone's medical records, contact info, or anything private. We use what's already on the internet — and find the patterns nobody else looks for.
My business isn't on the list. Can I still get a report?
Yes. Type your name + city in the search bar — if we don't have you yet, we'll add you and email when the report's ready (usually within 24 hours).
Will my competitors see my data?
Your dashboard is private. The data that *was already public anyway* (your Google reviews, your Yelp, your rating) appears in any agency searches that match your category. You can opt out anytime.
Can I cancel anytime?
One click. No phone call, no "are you sure" pop-ups. Monthly plans cancel at the end of the current cycle.
