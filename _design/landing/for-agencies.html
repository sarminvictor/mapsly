FAQ

Quick answers.

How is this different from Apollo, ZoomInfo, or Clay?
Those tools give you generic B2B contact data. Mapsly gives you local SMBs filtered by *intent signals* — what's broken on their site, who's running ads against them, whose reviews are tanking, who just rebuilt their site. Every lead comes with the reason to pitch, not just the contact.
How fresh are the contacts?
Every email is re-verified weekly. Phones daily on the Agency tier. If a contact bounces, we suppress it and surface a fresh one — at no extra cost.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. One click. Monthly plans cancel at end of period. Annual plans get prorated refund for unused months.
What about CAN-SPAM and CASL?
All contacts are publicly observable business emails (the same data on Google or Yelp). Mapsly stays on the right side of CAN-SPAM and CASL — but as the sender, you're responsible for compliant outreach. We include best-practice templates and a built-in suppression list to help.
Do you take a cut of deals I close?
No. Flat monthly subscription. Whatever you close is yours.
Affiliate program?
Yes. 30% recurring revenue for any SMB or agency signup you refer. Available to all paid agency tiers.
