Common questions

Quick answers.

Where does your data come from?
Google Maps, Google Search Console, Yelp, BBB, Trustpilot, Meta Ad Library, your website (Lighthouse), Reddit, job boards, certificate transparency, WHOIS, and 20+ other sources. Everything we use is either an official API or publicly observable. No scraping behind logins.
Is the free report really free?
Yes. One full report per business, no signup required. You see your rank, top 3 gaps, and your competitor breakdown. Paid plans unlock weekly refresh, alerts, more competitor detail, and the AI review-responder.
How is this different from other reputation tools?
Most tools tell you about your own reviews. Mapsly tells you how you compare to every competitor in your city — with the names, the reasons, and the fixes. No sales call. No annual contract. Cancel anytime.
What cities and verticals are covered?
At launch: med spa, HVAC, real estate, chiropractor, vet, roofing, plumbing, auto repair — across the top 30 metros in the US and Canada. Expanding to 65 metros and 20+ verticals through Year 1. You can request coverage of a vertical or city from inside the product.
Will my data be sold to anyone?
We use publicly observable signals only. When you sign up as a business, you opt into our network. Agencies see your business in lead searches if you match their criteria — that's the deal that funds your $39/mo. You can opt out of agency surfacing at any time.
