What is a one-pager?
What this is
A single-page cold-pitch brief
The one-pager is the artifact you attach to a cold email (or hand to a prospect at a meeting). One PDF page. Their business name, their visible problems, what you'd do, the projected impact.

It's not a deck. Not a proposal. Not a contract. It's the "here's what I noticed about your business" document that opens the conversation.
  • Single-page · always · print-ready
  • Tailored to one prospect, generated in < 10 seconds
  • Plain English · no jargon · for the owner, not the marketing manager
  • Loss-aversion top, gain-framing bottom
When to use it
Three workflows
1. Cold-email attachment. Reply rates jump 4–5× when there's a personalized PDF attached — recipient sees their name on the cover before they decide to read.

2. Pre-meeting send. "Before our call Tuesday, I wrote up what I saw. Look it over." Frames the meeting around your findings.

3. Walk-in / event hand-out. Print, fold. Industry conferences, trade shows, walk-in meetings — paper still wins.
