Overall
The strategy in one paragraph. Retie every research to ~$0.01 of our cost = 1 credit, so a credit costs us the same (~$0.012) no matter what it buys → margin is a flat 70–84% with no loss-making tail. Sell those credits in a 5-tier ladder (Free · $19 · $49 · $99 · $299) where $49 Solo is the real entry to advertise and $19/Free are organic land tiers. Keep "1 credit = 1 delivered local lead with contacts" as the honest anchor, strip the false claims (rollover, deep-audit), and lean the pitch on the local-signal moat + flat-vs-metered contrast against Origami/Apollo. The real risk is not unit cost — it's acquisition cost: $49+ pays back in 1–3 months, $19 doesn't, so never pay to acquire the cheap tiers.

A2 · Unified credit pricing — the whole point

With those 3 changes, cost per credit is uniform ~$0.012 for every research (was a $0.009→$0.06 spread). Margin no longer depends on what a credit buys — the loss-making tail is gone.

Before (today): Meta cost $0.12 for 4 credits = $0.03/credit ($0.06 with failures) — so on Growth/Pro/top-ups a Meta credit lost money. Reviews + walled Lighthouse were also thin. After: every research lands at $0.007–0.012/credit → flat margin.

A3 · The four costs we absorb — all now counted

Cost In the model? Size How it's handled
Discovery (map + refresh the index) ◐ + a guard ~$0.0015/lead · up to $1.20/big market Free users can't map (search reads the existing index; Target maps are paid-locked). But it's $0 to paying users & uncapped (3,000 businesses/market). Guard: cap depth ~500 on Free/Starter (decision F-8).
Failed research we pay for ✓ folded in ~15% uplift Baked into the $0.012/cr. Note: empty enrichment still charges the customer (decision D2) — only technical failures are our loss.
Free tier (50 credits × many) marketing line ~$0.50/free user Not a plan's margin. Capped by anti-abuse (1 grant per real email). ~$12.50 of free-cost per converted customer at 4% — trivial vs CAC.
Acquisition (ads / CAC) external assume ~$100/customer Not COGS — governs payback & LTV (Part B3). The real lever.
Blended cost per credit ≈ $0.012 = contacts $0.009 + discovery ~$0.0015 + failure loading, every research retied to ~$0.01. This one number drives all margins below.

  • Wire the 4 price ids (env, local + Vercel): 19→STRIPE_PRICE_PLAN_STARTER, 99→_GROWTH, 299→_SCALE, + a new slot for 49.
