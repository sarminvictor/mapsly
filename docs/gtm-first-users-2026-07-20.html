First 10–100 users — the seeded-market wedge

Everything below is built from what's actually in the code, the live database, and 2025–26 market data — not from what the docs claim. Constraints honored: no ads, no calls, automated-first, ~2–3 founder-hours/week, Claude does the heavy lifting.

The verdict in one paragraph. The problem isn't finding a channel — it's that every channel currently dumps visitors into a funnel that breaks at the wow moment. Free users can only search our existing index, and the index holds 6,630 businesses (not 2.1M), mostly Canadian dentists and beauty salons — a US agency's first search likely returns nothing. The fix: seed 10 US metro×vertical markets first, then cold-email only agencies whose market we've already analyzed — "we already ran your market, here's the proof" — using our own cold-email engine (which is genuinely good infrastructure that ran exactly one campaign, at SMBs, with the wrong copy). Free tool + programmatic SEO + community run in the background as compounding layers. Example: seed Denver dentists → email 45 Denver agencies "12 of 40 Denver dental practices load in 6+ seconds and never answer reviews — want the three worst, with contacts?" → reply gets a Proof Pack link + free account that is guaranteed non-empty. Simple numbers: ~250 fresh contacts/week ceiling, 1–4 signups/week expected once running → 10 external users by week 6–10, 100 total users around month 5–6 (cold email alone cannot produce 100 — the compounding layers must).

2Who to pitch — the ICP decision

Argued both ways by the red team; the call:

✅ Agency-first (Tom) — but only in seeded markets

  • Why: the product is built for him (homepage is already the agency landing), $49–$299 price carries real revenue at small user counts, he's reachable by email (it's literally his channel), and every successful analogue (Instantly, Smartlead, ListKit) got first users from the outbound world they sold into.
  • The constraint that makes it work: only email agencies whose metro×vertical we have already enriched, so the free signup is guaranteed a non-empty wow. The seeding step converts our fatal flaw (thin index) into the differentiator ("we already ran your market").
  • Caveat: agency owners are the most cold-email-saturated inbox in B2B (~2–4% reply typical). Only evidence-led copy clears their filter.

🔁 SMB (Maria) — the designated pivot, not the start

  • Why not first: $29/mo needs volume + trust we don't have; the SMB landing is demoted to /for-businesses; and our only SMB campaign produced 1 reply in 612.
  • Why it stays loaded: the index is SMB-native (dental/beauty), the cold copy engine and /l report are already SMB-shaped, so the pivot costs ~2 weeks, not a rebuild. If agencies fail the kill gates (§7), we flip.

3What to pitch and how

The offer (agency)

Not "try our SaaS". The offer is finished work: "We already analyzed your market. Here's evidence. A free account hands you 50 of these leads with verified contacts — no card, no call." This matches the 2026 best-practice pattern exactly (mini-audit / sample-list offers are what top-decile senders manufacture by hand — we generate them programmatically at ~$0 marginal cost).

Email 1 — plain text, no links, <80 words, binary ask

Subject: 12 dental sites in Denver load in 6+ seconds {{firstName}} — we ran a full scan of Denver's dental market last week. Of 40 practices: 12 have mobile sites loading 6s+, 9 haven't answered a review in 90 days, and 3 of their competitors are running Meta ads at them. Each one is a ready-made pitch for an agency like {{agencyName}}. Want the three worst offenders, with owner contacts? I'll just send them.

Rules baked in: no links or tracking pixel in email 1 (links cost 8–12% inbox placement), micro-batches under 50 per cell (doubles reply rates vs blasts), 3 touches over ~10 days max, US-only (CASL deferred), Proof Pack link + free-account link go in the reply or touch 2.

Reply → the Proof Pack moment

On any reply, send the agency-branded public Proof Pack link (/s/[token]) for a real business in their market + "your free account has the other 47 with contacts — takes 2 minutes, Google sign-in." The asset already exists in the product; it just has never been used as a sales weapon.

Community pitch (background lane)

r/agency or r/localseo post (monthly, no links, data in-post): "We audited all 533 med spas in our index: 61% never reply to reviews, median mobile load 5.2s, only 8% run any paid ads. If you sell to this vertical, the pitch writes itself — happy to share the method."

Value-first data drops are the only format that survives moderation (61% of relevant subreddits restrict or ban promo). Never automate posting — Reddit wiped ~70% of automated accounts in the 2025–26 crackdown. Monitoring automation (F5Bot, free) is safe.

SMB pitch (pivot path, already built)

The existing engine's angle: "your business, its actual problems, one report" via the /l briefing page. Held in reserve for Gate C (§7).

5The plan — 3 phases

Phase 0 · "Unbreak the funnel" — weeks 1–2 mostly Claude

Viktor's ~15 minutes (the only human-required items):

  • Verify STRIPE_PRICE_PLAN_STARTER/SOLO/GROWTH/SCALE are set in Vercel prod (otherwise the buy buttons are silently dead).
  • Grant the GSC service account access to the mapsly.ai property (~5 min).
  • Replace GA4_PROPERTY_ID (currently a GTM container ID) with the numeric GA4 property ID (~5 min). The tag is already collecting — history may become instantly queryable.
  • Buy domain #2 (e.g. getmapsly.com) with Google Workspace inboxes — the Zoho AUP hedge. (~$30/mo, 10 min; Claude preps everything else.)

Claude's work queue: fix free-CSV expectation (copy or in-app flag: "contacts in-app on Free"); point "Book a walkthrough" CTA at an async Proof Pack demo instead of /signin; soften landing claims to match code (74 signals; reword the 2.1M); add /for-agencies to sitemap + refresh lastmod; reset cold-inbox ramp + start warming both domains; write the agency copy branch (kill the hardcoded "med spas"); found-run the full product loop once end-to-end (move leads NEW→CONTACTED→WON, fire one OutreachEvent) to flush latent blockers before a stranger hits them.

Phase 1 · "Seeded-market wedge" — weeks 2–8 the bet

  • Seed 10 US metro×vertical cells (start with Miami med-spa — 261 rows + prior data already there; then e.g. Denver dental, Austin HVAC, Tampa law…). Run Target-mode discovery + enrichment internally. Cost: low single-digit dollars per cell.
  • Dogfood the agency list: run our own discovery on category "marketing agency" in those same metros (the index currently contains 1–3 agencies — we literally cannot prospect our ICP from our own data today). Agencies are local businesses; the signal engine works on them unchanged. Verify emails via the existing SMTP-probe path. Target: ≥500 verified agency contacts.
  • Send: micro-batches <50 per cell, 3 touches, plain-text link-free email 1, Proof Pack on reply/touch 2, US-only. Ceiling after re-ramp: ~750 sends ≈ 250 new contacts/week.
  • Founder's role: ~30 min/day reading replies and pasting Claude-drafted responses. Everything else is automated or Claude-run.

Phase 2 · Compounding background — starts week 2, runs forever Claude-driven

  • Free tool: "Grade any local business" — public read of existing BusinessSnapshot scoring, instant, $0 marginal cost, market-relative ("you rank 14th of 40 spas in Miami" — no live-scan competitor can say that). Email-capture before full report (HubSpot pattern: 30–40% capture). Later: embeddable white-label widget for agencies (proven model — MySiteAuditor, SEOptimer — and every embed is a backlink + an agency relationship).
  • Gated pSEO: publish /biz pages only for seeded rich-signal cells (hundreds, not thousands), fix sitemap, monitor indexation via restored GSC. Batches of 25–100 first; scale only at ≥80% indexation. Expect nothing before month 4 — that's fine, it's the 100-user engine for months 5–12.
  • Community lane (the manual 1–2 h/wk): F5Bot alerts on ~20 keywords; 2–3 genuinely helpful replies/week in r/agency + r/localseo; one monthly data-drop post; one data-study pitch/month to Near Media / LocalU / BrightLocal / Niche Pursuits (one accepted feature ≈ 20–100 qualified signups — the best yield-per-hour in the whole plan); apply to Online Geniuses Slack now (30-day vetting).
  • Directories: free G2/Capterra/SaaSHub/AlternativeTo listings once; ask early happy users for reviews (target 10 G2 + 5 Capterra = the LLM-citation threshold).
  • Launch pass (PH + Uneed + MicroLaunch): deferred to ~week 8–10, after activation is proven — median outcome ~115 mostly non-ICP signups; worth the backlinks, not worth 40 prep hours or a leaky funnel.

7Kill / pivot gates — MVP discipline

Prove it or fail fast. Each gate is a measurement, a threshold, and a pre-committed action. No gate may be renegotiated mid-flight.

GATE A · week 4 — Phase 0 not fully green (Stripe verified, GA4+GSC restored, ≥3 cells seeded, ≥500 verified agency contacts)? → Do not start sending. Fix or stop; sending into a broken funnel burns the list.
GATE B · first 500 delivered agency emails (~weeks 5–8) — bounce >5% → pause, re-verify list. Spam complaints visible in Postmaster → halt domain, switch to domain 2. Positive replies <0.5% OR free signups <3 → rewrite offer once, re-test 500. Second failure → kill agency cold email, keep compounding channels, trigger ICP review.
GATE C · week 8 — <10 external signups across ALL channels → begin the 4-week SMB-first cold test on the native index (US dental/beauty cells, existing copy engine).
GATE D · week 12 — <10 activated external users → stop outbound-led GTM entirely; go free-tool + SMB self-serve + pSEO only; reassess at month 6.
GATE E · month 6 — no paying external customer → the problem is product/offer, not channel. Stop GTM spend of any kind and revisit positioning.
STANDING — >50% of external signups hitting no_matches in any week → halt all sending until seeding catches up. Zoho suspension at any time → no resumption until migrated off.

8Best-practices cheat sheet (2025–26 rules)

Cold email

  • SPF + DKIM + DMARC aligned; RFC 8058 one-click unsub (we have it). Gmail hard-rejects non-compliant bulk since Nov 2025; Microsoft since May 2025.
  • Spam-complaint ceiling 0.3% — at 30 sends/day one click is 3.3%. Weekly Postmaster Tools check is mandatory (no automated loop exists).
  • 25–50/inbox/day max; 3–4 week warmup after idle; never send cold from mapsly.ai.
  • 3 touches over ~10 days captures ~93% of replies; touch 4+ triples unsubscribes.
  • No links/pixels in email 1; micro-batches <50 (5.8% vs 2.1% reply in published data); <80 words; one binary ask.
  • US-only for now. CASL = CAD $10M exposure, sender bears burden of proof; we have ConsentRecord plumbing but defer Canada.

Everything else

  • Reddit: value 70/30, data in-post, no links, never automate posting, never DM-solicit. Draft with AI, post by hand.
  • FB groups: weeks of participation before anything tool-shaped; admin relationships are the actual mechanism.
  • LinkedIn: personal profile only (3× reach vs company page); ≤50 manual invites/wk; DM only after engagement (~20% vs ~5% reply); no-note invites are fine — spend personalization on the post-accept DM.
  • pSEO: unique per-page signal data or don't publish; staged rollout; watch indexation ≥80% before scaling; local queries are 46–78% zero-click — target comparison/evidence intents.
  • Free tool: serve from snapshots ($0), rate-limit any live refresh, partial grade before email capture.
  • Metrics hygiene: exclude sarminvictor*, Boxly, and "Victor Sarmin 87" accounts from every funnel number (extends the secret-shopper rule).

9Top risks in the corrected plan

  • 1 · Domain/provider burn. One Zoho suspension (their AUP bans cold email — the one audit finding never fixed), a complaint spike (no FBL loop; auto-pause deliberately removed), or a bad scraped-list bounce tail zeroes the primary channel overnight. Mitigations: domain #2 on Google Workspace warming from week 1, email verification before enroll, weekly manual Postmaster checks. Reduced, not removed.
  • 2 · Activation is unproven. Zero external users have ever reached the wow moment. Seeding guarantees non-empty results — it does not guarantee agency owners find 50 free leads compelling enough to pay $49–99. The whole plan rests on an untested moment; Gate B exists precisely to test it cheaply.
  • 3 · Reply-rate reality. Our only empirical number (0.44%) sits at the industry floor. If the agency variant lands there too, 10 users takes ~6 months and patience runs out first. The Proof Pack lift is a hypothesis; 500 sends buys the answer.
  • 4 · Measurement blindness until Phase 0 lands. With GSC/GA4 broken and Stripe unverified, a silent funnel failure could burn weeks undetected. This is why Gate A blocks sending.
  • 5 · Claim drift. "2.1M businesses / 100+ signals" vs 6,630 / 74 in front of an ICP that audits claims for a living. Fix the copy before the first batch goes out.
