Add Poland as a third target market (US + Canada → + Poland)

Scope: agency "Discover"/Hunter market-research only — searching/filtering Polish businesses by signal. Cold outreach to Poland is explicitly out of scope (see §Legal).
Plain-English summary: nothing structural blocks this. The database already stores country as free text, the DataForSEO data pipeline already works per-country, and UI language is already decoupled from which country's businesses you can search — an agency can search Polish businesses in English today, no Polish translation needed. The real work is four concrete things: (1) one hardcoded allow-list (["US","CA"]) that currently blocks Poland from even being selectable, (2) a US/Canada-only city list that the live search feature depends on, (3) phone-number parsing that only understands North American numbers and would silently reject every Polish phone number, and (4) a plain-language legal note — collecting and displaying public Polish business data for research is on solid legal footing, but emailing those businesses is not (Poland requires opt-in consent, unlike the US), so that stays walled off. Estimated cost to seed an initial 2-city pilot: under $20.

§3Recommendations

  1. Ship the unblock work (§5 Phase 1) as one focused session — almost entirely mechanical (widen 3 type unions, fix 1 hardcoded enum, add 1 location-code case, fix 1 ternary + 2 copy strings) except the phone-parser replacement, which is real engineering.
  2. Treat the phone-number parser fix as the highest-leverage item in this whole plan. It's not Poland-specific debt — the 4 duplicated NANP-only parsers (modules/contacts/extract.ts:104-105,237-253, modules/contacts/reachability.ts:92-96, services/contact-scraper/parse.ts:163-172, services/lighthouse/dom-checks.ts:254-287) silently reject any non-North-American number today. Fixing it once pays off beyond this project.
  3. Add the cold-outreach eligibility gate in Phase 1, not as an afterthought. It's a small code change and it closes real legal exposure before a single Polish lead row exists — Poland's opt-in regime has no grace period the way CAN-SPAM's opt-out model does.
  4. Pilot 2 cities (Warsaw + Kraków) before committing to the full 6-metro rollout. Validates the entire pipeline against real Polish businesses and produces a real cost-per-business number — current estimates are order-of-magnitude only.
  5. Don't build hipaa_pixel_risk or license_status Poland-equivalents now. HIPAA has no direct analog (RODO is a different framework) and Poland has no "state licensing board" — it would need CEIDG/KRS + professional chamber (izba) lookups, real new scope with no signal yet that agencies need it. Suppress both for PL businesses; revisit if asked.
  6. Draft the Legitimate Interest Assessment + opt-out page early and in parallel with the engineering phases — it's your judgment call on language, not blocking code, and it's the one real compliance gap in the research-only scope.
  7. Re-verify DataForSEO's actual current per-call cost before the Phase 2 pilot spend. The confirmed ~20% global price increase (2026-07-01) means our existing budget assumptions may already be stale for US/CA too — worth a quick re-check regardless of Poland.
  8. Do not read this as unlocking Polish outreach. Discover's GDPR legitimate-interest basis for aggregating already-public data does not carry over to cold-emailing those businesses — Poland requires prior opt-in consent under a categorically different legal test (Art. 10 of the Act on Providing Services by Electronic Means + Art. 172 Telecommunications Law), enforced by UODO (up to 4% global turnover) and UKE.
