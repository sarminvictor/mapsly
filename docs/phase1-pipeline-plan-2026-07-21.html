Why in plain words: our mailer fills email templates with data about the recipient — but today it only knows how to look up SMBs from our Business table. Agencies aren't in that table, so their emails would render as "Hi there" with an empty evidence line. The fix: each agency recipient carries a small data bag ({firstName, agencyName, evidenceLine}) in a DB field that already exists but was never used — the edit (~5 lines) tells the mailer "also read this bag when filling the template." No schema change, zero risk to the SMB path. It must deploy because the mailer runs in production (Vercel cron), not on my machine.
